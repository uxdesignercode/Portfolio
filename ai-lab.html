Speech-to-text · LLM Extraction · Priority Scoring · CRM

Lost Races —
Lead Intelligence

The leads weren't dead. They'd been won by whoever called first and talked better. A brokerage was logging "not interested" and filing the loss as a dead lead — when it was actually a race they'd come fourth in.

I designed a system that extracts five structured fields from every call recording — motivation, capacity, constraints, engagement quality, stated preference — and feeds them into a priority queue the RM sees every morning. The follow-up card went from a clock counting minutes to a reading that says who is worth the call and why.

→ One extraction, five fields. Every downstream screen reads fields, not transcripts.
→ Before: 300 leads sorted by minutes elapsed. Neither clock said who was buying.
→ After: Three leads, ranked. One of them is worth the call right now.
→ Prototype stage. No shipped outcome metrics claimed.

Real-time · Manager View · AI Flags · Team Performance

Manager
Dashboard

A sales team of 30+ relationship managers, generating hundreds of calls a day — and the manager had no live view of any of it. Performance was invisible until someone pulled a report. By then the week was already lost.

The dashboard surfaces four live metrics — call volume, average call length, leads generated, AI-flagged conduct issues — and a per-RM bar chart that makes weak performance visible before it becomes a pattern. Flags carry surrounding context so managers can tell real issues from language-switching and regional phrasing.

→ AI flags shown with context — and a "Not an issue" action so managers don't start ignoring them.
→ Call volume, avg duration, leads, flags — four numbers that replace a weekly report.
→ RM scoring kept in two halves: call quality separate from conversion rate.

Automated · Broker-facing · Live Deal Data · Commission

AI Passbook
Calculator

Broker commission was calculated manually at month-end — by finance, from a spreadsheet, after every deal was reconciled by hand. Disputes were common. Trust was low. Brokers had no visibility into what they'd earned until the payout arrived.

The AI Passbook connects directly to closed deal data and calculates commission in real time — closures, site-visit bonuses, and lead quality incentives broken down line by line, with a progress bar toward the monthly target. No spreadsheet. No waiting until month-end. No surprises.

→ Three income streams calculated automatically: deal closures, site visit bonus, lead quality bonus.
→ Progress toward monthly target — visible to the broker at any point in the month.
→ Eliminates the reconciliation step that caused most commission disputes.

Lead Intelligence · AI Matching · Data Enrichment · Routing

Databank AI

5,240 leads sitting in the databank — not followed up, not filtered, not scored. Every RM stared at the same undifferentiated list and guessed. Whoever called first got credit, not whoever was most likely to convert.

Databank AI runs each lead through five scoring dimensions before any human touches it — property match, budget score, intent signal, location fit, and RM matching. The right lead surfaces to the right RM, and the queue the RM sees is already ranked. The guess is gone.

→ Five scoring dimensions, each with an evidence trail the RM can read and override.
→ RM matching factors past performance on similar lead profiles — not just availability.
→ Cold leads get periodic resurfacing on inbound signals: a missed call, a portal revisit, a message.
→ Community-based routing explicitly excluded. The line is whether the client said it.
