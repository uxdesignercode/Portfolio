What changed

Same shell. The screens now say why.

The overview tiles, the four time slots and the card layout are unchanged — RMs already know this app. The additions sit inside what was already there:

  • On the overview, a line that turns counts into a plan. Tiles say how much work; this says which work.
  • On each card, a ranking reason, motivation and capacity tags extracted rather than typed, and the transcript line behind the reading.
  • In the log-call sheet, a comment drafted from the call and a suggested slot with its reason — editing instead of typing.

The stage label stays. It answers a different question — where the lead sits in the pipeline, not whether he's buying.

Tap a tile to open the list. Tap a card for the brief and its call transcripts. Tap the blue phone button to see the after-call sheet.
